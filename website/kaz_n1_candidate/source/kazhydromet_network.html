Hydrological Monitoring

 

The hydrological network consists of 442 observation posts (381 permanent, 61 seasonal), including: river post – 394 (make observations on 228 rivers, 1 stream, 3 channels); lake post – 38 (make observations on 20 lakes, 7 reservoirs); marine post – 7; marine hydrometeorological station – 3, (make observations at sea). For more detailed information, see the 'Annotation' section.
